READ BEFORE GRADING

How this view should be used

  • Threat means a rusher is within 2.5 yards of the moving QB; it is an exploratory reference, not an official pressure definition.
  • Pocket shading is an explainable triangle between the QB and outside core blockers, not an official pocket boundary.
  • Use assignments as an initial matchup only. Stunts, switches, chips, free rushers, and QB movement still require film review.
  • All plays are flipped so the offense moves right. Lane labels are always stated from the offense's perspective first.
